Noi WriteRight vao UNICOACH LMS: SSO vao + day diem ve

Truoc day LMS da dung san khung tich hop (bang linked_apps, route phat
token SSO, POST /api/v1/results) nhung phia WriteRight chua co gi. Commit
nay lam not phia WriteRight.

lib/unicoach.ts (moi)
- verifyLmsToken(): xac thuc JWT HS256 do LMS phat. Kiem dinh dang, THUAT
  TOAN (chan alg=none), chu ky so sanh timing-safe, han dung, iss, aud, sub.
  Co y KHONG them thu vien jwt — chi can verify HS256 nen tu viet bang
  node:crypto, tranh them dependency va rui ro lockfile khi build Vercel.
- lmsEmailFor(): dung email that neu hop le, khong thi sinh email noi bo
  on dinh theo ma hoc vien (vd hv001@lms.unicoach.vn) — vi LMS nhieu khi
  chi luu email phu huynh, hoac hoc vien chua co email.
- pushResultToLms(): goi POST {LMS}/api/v1/results kem X-API-Key. Timeout
  8s, KHONG BAO GIO nem loi ra ngoai.

app/sso/route.ts (moi) — LMS mo /sso?token=...
  Xac thuc token -> tim/tao user Supabase qua service role -> luu
  lms_student_code vao user_metadata -> sinh magic link -> chuyen sang
  /auth/confirm de dat cookie phien. Loi thi ve /login kem thong bao.
  runtime nodejs (can node:crypto).

app/auth/confirm/route.ts (moi) — doi token_hash lay phien va dat cookie.
  Tach rieng khoi /auth/callback (cai do xu ly ma OAuth Google).
  Chan open redirect: chi cho chuyen huong noi bo.

app/api/evaluate/route.ts — sau khi luu evaluations, neu user co
  lms_student_code thi day band + 4 tieu chi + nhan xet ve LMS. Dung
  evaluations.id lam external_id nen LMS chong trung san. Chay kieu
  "ban va quen": LMS sap hay chua cau hinh cung khong hong viec cham bai.

.env.example: them UNICOACH_SSO_SECRET, UNICOACH_LMS_URL, UNICOACH_API_KEY.
